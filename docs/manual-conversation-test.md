# One-way conversation test

Start a fresh session. Use your actual full name in the introduction. Keep recording throughout; leave a brief natural pause between answers.

**Friend:** “I'm Alex. What are you working on?”

**Builder:** “I'm [your full name]. I'm building a car marketplace called Roadnest. It helps buyers compare discounted dealer stock.”

**Builder:** “It also sends WhatsApp price-drop alerts. Users can book test drives.”

**Friend:** “Could this work for fitness coaches too?”

**Builder:** “Separately, I'm building a CAD review tool called MeshPilot. It checks models before they go to a 3D printer.”

**Builder:** “I added an invoicing feature to Roadnest.”

**Builder:** “Mesh Pilot also checks thin walls and missing clearances.”

Expected results:

- Your full name owns the portfolio; Alex's introduction does not take it over.
- Exactly two projects: Roadnest and MeshPilot.
- WhatsApp alerts, booking and invoicing stay under Roadnest.
- Thin-wall and clearance checks stay under MeshPilot. The spoken spacing variation does not rename or duplicate it.
- The fitness question does not create a project or invent an implemented feature.
- Recording continues while the portfolio updates, with no questions or confirmation UI.

These names are spoken test inputs, not generator defaults. Wipe should remove the entire session and its remembered identities.
